CertTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

CertTrack is designed to protect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

CertTrack collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What data the app stores

CertTrack stores the certification information you enter — such as names, issuing organizations, dates, notes, and any documents you attach. This information is stored:

  • On your device, in the app's private local storage, and
  • In your personal iCloud account (via Apple's CloudKit), if you leave iCloud Sync enabled, so your certifications stay in sync across your Apple devices.

This data never leaves your device or your own iCloud account. The developer cannot see it, and it is never transmitted to the developer or to any third-party server.

iCloud Sync

If iCloud Sync is enabled (the default), your certifications are stored in the private database of your own iCloud account and synced across your devices by Apple. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time in the app's Settings, in which case your data stays only on the current device.

Notifications

CertTrack schedules local notifications on your device to remind you before a certification expires. These are generated entirely on-device and are not sent through any server.

Data the app does NOT collect

  • No name, email, or contact information
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No third-party SDKs or trackers

Children's privacy

CertTrack does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
