CertTrack Support

Thanks for using CertTrack! This page answers common questions. For anything else, email certtrack@icloud.com.

Getting started

  • Tap + on the Certificates screen to add a certification. Enter at least a name and an expiration date.
  • Certificates are grouped automatically into Expired, Expiring Soon, and Active.
  • Tap any certificate to see its details, attach a document, or edit it.
  • Use the Timeline tab to see all your credentials on one time axis.

Reminders

  • Set per-certificate reminders (90 / 60 / 30 / 14 / 7 / 3 / 1 days before expiration) when adding or editing a certificate.
  • Notifications are delivered at 9:00 AM on each reminder day.
  • If you're not seeing notifications, make sure notifications are allowed for CertTrack in Settings → Notifications → CertTrack.

"Expiring Soon" window

You can change how many days before expiration a certificate is flagged as "Expiring Soon" in the app's Settings → Preferences.

iCloud Sync

  • CertTrack syncs your certifications across your devices using your own iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device and that iCloud Drive is enabled.
  • You can turn sync on or off in Settings → iCloud Sync. Changes take effect after restarting the app.
  • If sync shows an error, confirm you're signed in to iCloud and connected to the internet, then tap Sync Now.

Documents

  • You can attach a PDF or image (JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF) to any certificate.
  • Attachments are limited to 25 MB each so they sync reliably.

Apple Shortcuts

CertTrack provides three Shortcuts actions: Add Certificate, Get Expiring Certificates, and Check Certificate Status. Find them in the Shortcuts app under CertTrack.

Contact

Still stuck? Email certtrack@icloud.com and include your device model and iOS/macOS version.
